# Notelert Plugin v1.1.10

This release brings the public plugin in line with Notelert's production-ready account, email, and billing flows.

## Highlights

- **Account and email controls:** email verification, account status, data export, account deletion, and installation revocation are available from the plugin settings.
- **Reliable reminder delivery:** updated scheduling for email and Android reminder channels, including clearer validation and error feedback.
- **Premium billing:** localized Monthly and Yearly Checkout, Customer Portal access, stable loading buttons, and a safe return path back to Obsidian after billing.
- **Account linking:** improved app-link token handling to keep the plugin and Android app synchronized under one Notelert account.
- **Compatibility and review quality:** migrated UI creation to Obsidian helpers, removed lint findings, and preserved compatibility with Obsidian 1.8.7 and later.
